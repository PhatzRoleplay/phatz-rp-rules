OFFICIAL COMMUNITY RULES

Welcome to Phatz RP

Phatz RP is focused on serious, story-driven roleplay. These rules are here to keep roleplay fair, believable, and enjoyable for everyone.

Whether you are experienced with FiveM or completely new, read through the rules before getting started.

01

Community Rules

Basic rules that apply across the Phatz RP community.

COM-001

Respect the Community

Treat players and staff with respect. Harassment, bullying, discrimination and targeted abuse are not allowed.

COM-002

Staff Decisions

If you disagree with a staff decision, stay respectful and use the correct appeal or report process.

02

Roleplay Rules

Rules covering realistic and fair roleplay.

RP-001

Metagaming

Do not use information your character did not learn through roleplay.

RP-002

Powergaming

Do not abuse game mechanics or force unrealistic outcomes onto another player.

RP-003

Fail RP

Your character must react realistically to injuries, danger and the situation around them.

03

Criminal Roleplay

Rules covering crime, hostages and hostile situations.

CRIM-001

Hostages

Hostages must be taken naturally through roleplay and cannot be pre-arranged.

CRIM-002

Player Robbing

Players may not rob other players or search vehicle boots for items.

04

Crew Rules

Rules for registered crews and unregistered groups.

CREW-001

Registered Crew Hostilities

A registered crew may have up to 7 official members involved in a hostile situation.

CREW-002

Unregistered Groups

Unregistered groups may have no more than 5 people involved in a hostile situation.

CREW-003

Gang Beef

Gang conflict must build naturally through roleplay and should not immediately turn into large shootouts.

05

Reports & Compensation

Information about reporting players and claiming compensation.

REP-001

Compensation Evidence

Compensation claims require video evidence. No clip means no compensation.

06

Definitions

Common FiveM and roleplay terms.

TERM

RDM

Random Deathmatch — seriously injuring or killing someone without enough roleplay leading up to it.

TERM

VDM

Vehicle Deathmatch — intentionally using a vehicle as a weapon against another player.
