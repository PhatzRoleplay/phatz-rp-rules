OFFICIAL COMMUNITY RULES

Welcome to Phatz RP

Phatz RP is a serious, story-first roleplay server. We want players to build characters, create stories and enjoy the consequences of both winning and losing.

If you are new to FiveM, that is okay. Read the rules, use common sense and ask staff when you are unsure. Genuine mistakes can be taught. Deliberate rule breaks and repeat behaviour will be treated more seriously.

01

Community

How we expect people to act across Phatz RP.

COM

Community Conduct

Respect people, keep conflict in RP, and do not bring harmful behaviour into the community.

COM-001 Respect Everyone

Treat players and staff with respect.

  • No harassment, bullying or targeted abuse.
  • No excessive toxicity or real-life threats.
  • Do not use RP as an excuse to attack the player behind the character.
COM-002 Hate Speech & Discrimination

Racist, homophobic, sexist or other hateful slurs and behaviour are not allowed.

Example: Saying something is “just your character” does not make hate speech okay.

COM-003 No ERP

Erotic or explicit sexual roleplay is not allowed.

  • No sexual harassment.
  • No sexual assault roleplay.
  • No explicit sexual scenes.
COM-004 Staff Decisions & Appeals

Follow staff instructions during an active situation. If you disagree, use the correct appeal or staff report process.

  • Do not argue about punishments in public channels.
  • Do not lie to staff during an investigation.
  • Stay respectful even when you disagree.
COM-005 Advertising

Do not advertise or recruit for other FiveM servers or competing communities.

Phatz RP content creators may promote content that is related to Phatz RP.

COM-006 Real-World Gangs & Extremist Groups

Do not directly copy, portray or promote real-world gangs, cartels, terrorist groups, extremist groups or hate groups.

Create original fictional organisations instead.

COM-007 Common Sense & Staff Discretion

Not every situation can be written into a rulebook. Do not look for loopholes.

Staff may act on behaviour that clearly harms the server and will consider intent, context, severity, player history and the effect on others.

02

Roleplay

Core standards for serious, fair and believable RP.

RP

Roleplay Standards

Stay in character, act realistically and give everyone a fair chance to roleplay.

RP-001 Stay In Character

Stay in character while in-game unless staff clearly move the conversation OOC.

  • Do not talk about rules, keybinds, Discord messages or staff actions inside normal RP.
  • Keep real-world problems outside the scene.
RP-002 Metagaming

Do not use information your character did not learn through roleplay.

  • No Discord or DM information.
  • No stream or clip information.
  • No knowledge from another character you play.
  • No outside app communication for IC information.
RP-003 Voice ID

You cannot identify a masked player by their voice alone.

Use instead: clothing, tattoos, vehicles or other clues your character can properly recognise.

RP-004 Powergaming

Do not force unrealistic outcomes or abuse game mechanics for an advantage.

  • Give other players a fair chance to react.
  • Do not use camera angles, menus, emotes or mechanics in ways they were not intended.
RP-005 Fear RP & Valuing Life

Act like your character wants to stay alive.

  • React realistically to weapons.
  • React realistically when heavily outnumbered.
  • Do not behave as if your character is fearless or invincible.
RP-006 Injuries & Fail RP

Roleplay injuries properly. If needed, use /me to show your physical state.

  • Do not ignore serious injuries.
  • Do not instantly sprint away after treatment as if nothing happened.
  • Failure to roleplay injuries may be treated as Fail RP.
RP-007 No Intent to Roleplay

Join the server to roleplay, not just to find action.

  • Do not pretend to be AFK to avoid RP.
  • Do not repeatedly create low-effort conflict.
  • Do not treat the server like a shooter.
RP-008 Story Over Winning

You do not need to win every situation. Accept losses and consequences when they happen.

Good RP is about creating a story, not always coming out on top.

RP-009 RDM & VDM

RDM: Do not seriously injure or kill someone without enough RP leading up to it.

VDM: Do not deliberately use a vehicle as a weapon to injure or kill someone.

Remember: violence should come from the story, not be the first answer to a minor problem.

RP-010 Baiting, Trolling & Griefing

Do not provoke people just to force a chase, fight or shootout.

  • No cop baiting.
  • No gang baiting.
  • No mic spam, emote spam or deliberate disruption.
RP-011 Combat Logging

Do not disconnect to avoid an active RP situation or its consequences.

If you genuinely crash or have an emergency, return when possible and contact staff if needed.

RP-012 New Life Rule

If you respawn after dying, you forget the events that directly caused your death.

  • You cannot be reminded by other players.
  • You cannot return to the scene to continue the same situation.
  • If revived through RP, you may remember what happened but cannot re-enter or continue the conflict.
RP-013 Downed Roleplay

While downed, roleplay your injuries and physical state.

Do not act normally or ignore the condition your character is in. Use /me where needed to show injuries that cannot be seen.

RP-014 Cheating, Exploiting & Advantage Mods

Do not use cheats, hacks, DMA, ESP, macros, exploits, bugs or modified settings that give an unfair advantage.

  • No custom combat advantages.
  • No visibility mods such as removed foliage or no-night packs.
  • No unfair crosshairs, tracers or audio changes.
  • Report exploits instead of using them.
RP-015 Real Money Trading

Do not buy, sell, trade or gift in-game assets in exchange for real-world money, gift cards, cryptocurrency or anything else of real-world value.

03

Criminal RP

Rules for crime, hostages and hostile scenes.

CRIM

Criminal Roleplay

Crime should create stories, not remove roleplay from other players.

CRIM-001 Criminal RP Must Create a Story

Criminal RP should create interaction and story. Do not treat crime as a reason to constantly shoot, chase money or skip roleplay.

CRIM-002 No Player Robbing

You may not rob another player or take items, money, weapons or other property from their inventory.

This applies whether the player is alive, downed or restrained.

CRIM-003 No Boot Checking

Do not check or search another player's vehicle boot or trunk for items.

This applies whether the vehicle is occupied or unattended.

CRIM-004 No Scamming

Player scamming is not allowed.

Do not use RP, trades, deals or game systems to trick another player out of money, items, vehicles or other assets.

CRIM-005 Hostages & Kidnapping

Hostages must be taken naturally through RP.

  • No pre-planned or willing hostages.
  • You may know the person, but they cannot agree beforehand.
  • A hostage may be held for a maximum of 30 minutes.
  • Kidnapping must have a real RP reason.
CRIM-006 Emergency Services

Do not target police, EMS or other emergency workers for no reason.

You cannot take issued equipment or other items from them because player robbing is not allowed.

CRIM-007 Unregistered Group Hostile Limit

An unregistered group may have no more than 5 people involved in the same hostile situation.

You cannot use extra friends, lookouts or outside players to get around the limit.

04

Crews

Registered crew rules, beef and gang wars.

CREW

Crew & Gang Rules

Crews should build long-term RP, not turn every problem into a shootout.

CREW-001 Crew Size & Roster
  • Minimum 4 official members.
  • Maximum 7 official members.
  • Maximum 2 approved hangarounds.
  • Keep the crew roster up to date.
CREW-002 Hostile Limits

All 7 official crew members may take part in the same hostile situation.

Hangarounds cannot take part in hostilities, gang beef fights or gang wars.

CREW-003 Leaving & Joining Crews

After leaving or being removed from a crew, you must wait 14 days before joining another crew.

Do not try to bypass the cooldown.

CREW-004 RP Comes First

Crews are expected to create events, relationships, rivalries and storylines.

A crew should not exist only to shoot, rob or start fights.

CREW-005 Gang Beef Must Build Naturally

Gang beef must start from real RP such as an argument, disrespect, territory issue, kidnapping or another believable problem.

Do not force beef because you want a fight.

CREW-006 Escalation

Conflict should normally build from smaller encounters into more serious ones.

  • Threats and arguments.
  • Kidnappings or smaller confrontations.
  • Melee or smaller armed encounters.
  • Larger shootouts only when the RP has built to that point.
CREW-007 Convoys & Weapons

Do not bring the entire crew to every small issue.

Match your weapons to the level of conflict. Do not jump straight to the strongest weapons for every disagreement.

CREW-008 Do Not Wake Members Up for Active Beef

Do not message crew members OOC and tell them to join the server just to take part in an active conflict.

This may be treated as metagaming and No Intent to RP.

CREW-009 Gang Wars

Gang wars must come from proper RP build-up.

During an active gang war, participating members must be in their crew's Combat VC and remain deafened so Discord cannot be used for IC communication.

CREW-010 Crew Discipline

Staff may warn, restrict, remove or disband a crew if its members repeatedly break rules, abuse systems or damage the RP environment.

05

Reports

Evidence, staff reports and compensation.

REP

Reports & Compensation

Give staff enough evidence to make a fair decision and keep active matters private.

REP-001 Player Reports

Reports must include enough evidence to show the full situation.

Example: An 8-second clip showing only the final shot may not be enough to prove RDM.

REP-002 Staff Reports

Use the correct staff report process and provide evidence that clearly supports the complaint.

REP-003 Keep Active Reports Private

Do not publicly argue about or share details from an active report, ticket or appeal.

Let the staff team handle it through the proper process.

REP-004 No Clip, No Compensation

Compensation claims require video evidence. Screenshots alone are not accepted.

If you do not have a clip showing what happened, compensation will not be given.

REP-005 Compensation Examples

Compensation may be considered for things such as:

  • Server rollback.
  • Script fault.
  • Character data problem.
  • Bug-related death.
  • Items disappearing because of a bug.
  • Missing paid items.
  • Vehicle deletion.
  • Server crash during an active scenario.
REP-006 How Punishments Are Decided

Staff will consider:

  • How serious the breach was.
  • Whether it was intentional.
  • Previous player history.
  • How other players were affected.
  • Whether the player cooperated.

Genuine new-player mistakes may be explained first. Serious or deliberate breaches can skip straight to stronger punishment.

06

Definitions

Quick FiveM terms for newer players.

TERM

Common Terms

Short definitions for words used throughout the rules.

IC — In Character

Anything your character says, does, knows or experiences inside the roleplay world.

OOC — Out of Character

Anything outside the roleplay world, such as Discord messages, streams, real-life conversations or staff discussions.

RDM — Random Deathmatch

Seriously injuring or killing someone without enough roleplay leading up to it.

VDM — Vehicle Deathmatch

Deliberately using a vehicle as a weapon to injure or kill another player.

NLR — New Life Rule

The rule that controls what your character remembers and can do after dying and respawning.

Metagaming

Using information in-character that your character did not properly learn through roleplay.

Powergaming

Using unrealistic actions, forced outcomes or game mechanics to gain an unfair advantage.

Fear RP

The expectation that your character values their life and reacts realistically to danger.

Fail RP

Roleplay that seriously ignores realism, injuries, consequences or the actions of other players.

Combat Logging

Disconnecting from the server to escape an active RP situation or its consequences.

NITRP — No Intent to Roleplay

Behaviour that shows a player is not making a real effort to take part in roleplay.

RMT / RWT

Real Money Trading — exchanging in-game assets for real-world money or anything else with real-world value.
