OFFICIAL COMMUNITY RULES

Welcome to Phatz RP

Phatz RP is a serious, story-first roleplay server. We want players to build characters, create stories and enjoy the consequences of both winning and losing.

If you are new to FiveM, that is okay. Read the rules, use common sense and ask staff when you are unsure. Genuine mistakes can be taught. Deliberate rule breaks and repeat behaviour will be treated more seriously.

01

Community Rules

Rules that apply across the Phatz RP community.

COM-001

Respect Everyone

Treat players and staff with respect. Harassment, bullying, discrimination, targeted abuse, excessive toxicity and real-life threats are not allowed.

COM-002

No Hate Speech

Racist, homophobic, sexist or other hateful slurs and behaviour are not allowed, even if you claim it was a joke or part of your character.

COM-003

No ERP

Erotic roleplay and explicit sexual roleplay are not allowed. Sexual harassment and sexual assault roleplay are also not allowed.

COM-004

Staff Decisions & Appeals

Follow staff instructions during an active situation. If you disagree with a decision, stay respectful and use the proper appeal or staff report process. Do not argue about punishments in public channels or lie to staff during an investigation.

COM-005

No Advertising

Do not advertise or recruit for other FiveM servers or competing communities. Phatz RP content creators may promote content that is related to Phatz RP.

COM-006

No Real-World Gangs or Extremist Groups

Do not directly copy, portray or promote real-world gangs, cartels, terrorist groups, extremist groups or hate groups. Use original fictional organisations instead.

COM-007

Use Common Sense

The rules cannot cover every possible situation. Do not look for loopholes. Act in good faith and choose the option that creates better roleplay for everyone involved.

COM-008

Staff Discretion

Staff may act on behaviour that clearly harms the server even if the exact situation is not written word-for-word in the rules. Staff will consider intent, context, severity, player history and the effect on others.

02

Roleplay Rules

Core rules for realistic, fair and story-focused roleplay.

RP-001

Stay In Character

Stay in character while in-game unless staff clearly move the conversation OOC. Do not talk about rules, keybinds, staff actions, Discord messages or other game mechanics inside normal RP.

RP-002

No Metagaming

Do not use information your character did not learn through roleplay. This includes Discord, streams, clips, messages, another character you play, or information passed to you OOC.

RP-003

Keep IC Communication In-Game

In-character information must be shared through in-game voice, phone or radio. Do not use Discord calls or other outside apps to communicate IC information.

RP-004

No Powergaming

Do not force unrealistic outcomes or abuse game mechanics to gain an advantage. Give other players a fair chance to react and take part in the RP.

RP-005

No Voice ID

You cannot identify a masked player by their voice alone. Use realistic clues such as clothing, tattoos, vehicles or other information your character can properly recognise.

RP-006

Value Your Life

Act like your character wants to stay alive. React realistically to weapons, dangerous situations and being heavily outnumbered. Do not behave as if your character is fearless or invincible.

RP-007

Fail RP & Injuries

Roleplay injuries properly. If needed, use /me to show your physical state. Ignoring serious injuries, immediately sprinting away after treatment or acting as if nothing happened may be treated as Fail RP.

RP-008

No Intent to Roleplay

Join the server to roleplay. Do not ignore scenarios, pretend to be AFK to avoid RP, repeatedly create low-effort conflict or behave as if the server is only a shooter.

RP-009

Story Over Winning

You do not need to win every situation. Accept losses and consequences when they happen. Good RP is about creating a story, not always coming out on top.

RP-010

No RDM

Do not seriously injure or kill another player without enough RP leading up to it. Violence should come from the story and should not be the first answer to a minor problem.

RP-011

No VDM

Do not deliberately use a vehicle as a weapon to injure or kill another player. Normal vehicle contact during realistic driving or pursuits will be judged by context.

RP-012

No Baiting

Do not provoke players, gangs or police just to force a chase, fight or shootout. Conflict should come from real RP, not because you are looking for action.

RP-013

No Trolling or Griefing

Do not deliberately ruin another player's RP through trolling, harassment, repeated disruption, mic spam, emote spam or other behaviour that exists only to annoy people.

RP-014

No Combat Logging

Do not disconnect to avoid an active RP situation or its consequences. If you genuinely crash or have an emergency, return when possible and contact staff if needed.

RP-015

New Life Rule

If you respawn after dying, you forget the events that directly caused your death. You cannot be reminded by other players and cannot return to the scene to continue the same situation. If you are revived through RP, you may remember what happened but cannot re-enter or continue the conflict.

RP-016

Downed Roleplay

While downed, roleplay your injuries and physical state. Do not act normally or ignore the condition your character is in. Use /me where needed to show injuries that cannot be seen.

RP-017

No Cheating

Cheats, hacks, aim assistance, ESP, triggerbots, DMA use, macros or any other third-party tool that gives an unfair advantage are not allowed and may result in a permanent ban.

RP-018

No Exploiting

Do not abuse bugs, glitches or unintended mechanics for an advantage. If you find an exploit, stop using it and report it to staff.

RP-019

No Advantage Mods

Do not use graphics packs, crosshairs, audio changes or settings that give you an unfair combat or visibility advantage. Normal cosmetic graphics mods are fine only if they do not change gameplay fairness.

RP-020

No Real Money Trading

Do not buy, sell, trade or gift in-game money, items, vehicles, accounts, businesses or other assets in exchange for real-world money, gift cards, cryptocurrency or anything else of real-world value.

03

Criminal Roleplay

Rules for hostilities, crime, hostages and criminal scenes.

CRIM-001

Criminal RP Must Create a Story

Criminal RP should create interaction and story. Do not treat crime as a reason to constantly shoot, chase money or skip roleplay.

CRIM-002

No Player Robbing

You may not rob another player or take items, money, weapons or other property from their inventory. This applies regardless of whether the other player is alive, downed or restrained.

CRIM-003

No Boot Checking

Do not check or search another player's vehicle boot or trunk for items. This applies whether the vehicle is occupied or unattended.

CRIM-004

No Scamming

Player scamming is not allowed. Do not use RP, trades, deals or game systems to trick another player out of money, items, vehicles or other assets.

CRIM-005

Hostages Must Be Natural

Hostages cannot be pre-planned, paid, volunteered or arranged beforehand. You may know the person, but they cannot agree in advance to become your hostage. A hostage may be held for a maximum of 30 minutes.

CRIM-006

Kidnapping

Kidnapping must have a real RP reason. A kidnapped player may not be held for more than 30 minutes unless staff approve a longer story beforehand.

CRIM-007

Emergency Services

Do not target police, EMS or other emergency workers for no reason. You also cannot take issued equipment or any other items from them because player robbing is not allowed.

CRIM-008

Unregistered Group Hostile Limit

An unregistered group may have no more than 5 people involved in the same hostile situation. You cannot use extra friends, lookouts or outside players to get around this limit.

04

Crew Rules

Rules for registered crews, hangarounds, beef and gang wars.

CREW-001

Crew Size

A registered crew must have at least 4 official members and may have up to 7 official members. A crew may also have up to 2 approved hangarounds. Keep the crew roster up to date.

CREW-002

Registered Crew Hostile Limit

All 7 official crew members may take part in the same hostile situation. Hangarounds cannot take part in hostilities.

CREW-003

Hangarounds

Hangarounds may be part of the wider crew setup, but they cannot join crew hostilities, gang beef fights or gang wars.

CREW-004

Leaving & Joining Crews

After leaving or being removed from a crew, you must wait 14 days before joining another crew. Do not try to bypass the cooldown.

CREW-005

RP Comes First

Crews are expected to create events, relationships, rivalries and storylines. A crew should not exist only to shoot, rob or start fights.

CREW-006

Gang Beef Must Build Naturally

Gang beef must start from real RP such as an argument, disrespect, territory issue, kidnapping or another believable problem. Do not force beef because you want a fight.

CREW-007

Escalate Naturally

Conflict should normally build from smaller encounters into more serious ones. Threats, arguments, kidnappings and smaller fights should come before large shootouts when the story allows it.

CREW-008

Do Not Full-Convoy Every Situation

Do not bring the entire crew to every small problem. Large convoys should make sense for the stage of the conflict and the RP that led to it.

CREW-009

Match Weapons to the Situation

Use weapons that make sense for the level of conflict. Do not jump straight to the strongest weapons available for every disagreement. Taking a loss is part of RP.

CREW-010

Do Not Wake Members Up for Active Beef

Do not message crew members OOC and tell them to join the server just to take part in an active conflict. This may be treated as metagaming and No Intent to RP.

CREW-011

Gang Wars

Gang wars must come from proper RP build-up. During an active gang war, participating members must be in their crew's Combat VC and remain deafened so Discord cannot be used for IC communication.

CREW-012

Crew Discipline

Staff may warn, restrict, remove or disband a crew if its members repeatedly break rules, abuse systems or damage the RP environment.

05

Reports & Compensation

How to report problems and request compensation.

REP-001

Player Reports

Reports must include enough evidence to show the full situation. A short clip that only shows the ending may not be enough for staff to make a fair decision.

REP-002

Staff Reports

If you need to report a staff member, use the correct staff report process and provide evidence that clearly supports the complaint.

REP-003

Keep Active Reports Private

Do not publicly argue about or share details from an active report, ticket or appeal. Let the staff team handle it through the proper process.

REP-004

No Clip, No Compensation

Compensation claims require video evidence. Screenshots alone are not accepted. If you do not have a clip showing what happened, compensation will not be given.

REP-005

When Compensation May Be Given

Compensation may be considered for things such as server rollbacks, script faults, character data problems, bug-related deaths, items disappearing because of a bug, missing paid items, vehicle deletion or a server crash during an active scenario. Staff will review the clip and decide.

REP-006

How Punishments Are Decided

Staff will consider the seriousness of the breach, intent, previous history, effect on other players and whether the player cooperated. Genuine new-player mistakes may be explained first, while serious or deliberate breaches can skip straight to stronger punishment.

06

Definitions

Common FiveM and roleplay terms used in these rules.

TERM

IC — In Character

Anything your character says, does, knows or experiences inside the roleplay world.

TERM

OOC — Out of Character

Anything outside the roleplay world, such as Discord messages, streams, real-life conversations or staff discussions.

TERM

RDM — Random Deathmatch

Seriously injuring or killing someone without enough roleplay leading up to it.

TERM

VDM — Vehicle Deathmatch

Deliberately using a vehicle as a weapon to injure or kill another player.

TERM

NLR — New Life Rule

The rule that controls what your character remembers and can do after dying and respawning.

TERM

Metagaming

Using information in-character that your character did not properly learn through roleplay.

TERM

Powergaming

Using unrealistic actions, forced outcomes or game mechanics to gain an unfair advantage.

TERM

Fear RP

The expectation that your character values their life and reacts realistically to danger.

TERM

Fail RP

Roleplay that seriously ignores realism, injuries, consequences or the actions of other players.

TERM

Combat Logging

Disconnecting from the server to escape an active RP situation or its consequences.

TERM

NITRP — No Intent to Roleplay

Behaviour that shows a player is not making a real effort to take part in roleplay.

TERM

RMT / RWT

Real Money Trading — exchanging in-game assets for real-world money or anything else with real-world value.
